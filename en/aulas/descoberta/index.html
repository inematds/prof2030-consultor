Lesson 2 · 20 min

The discovery conversation

Nobody leaves the doctor's office with a prescription before the exam. In AI consulting, this happens all the time: the owner describes a pain and the consultant already answers with a tool. In 45 minutes of a well-run conversation, you walk out with the bottleneck, a number for today, and the list of what's already running in the shadows of the company.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The 45-minute script, block by block
  2. 2Turning pain into a number: the baseline
  3. 3Finding the AI your employees already use without IT knowing
  4. 4Asking for data without creating an LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) problem

Goal: run a 45-minute discovery conversation and walk out with the bottleneck, the baseline, and the inventory of AI in use.

  1. 1/7

    Opening

    Three sentences: the goal ("to understand where the work gets stuck"), the prep ("I looked at your website and reviews"), and the word handed back ("I wanted to hear it in your own words"). Ask permission to take notes.

  2. 2/7

    The path of the order

    "Tell me, step by step, what happens from the moment the customer places an order until the money comes in." Write down each step, who does it, and where it gets stuck. Ask for a real case from last week, not the rule.

  3. 3/7

    The bottleneck

    Where queues form, where work gets redone, where customers get lost. For each pain, ask for the number: how many per week, how long, how much it costs. A pain without a number is still an opinion.

  4. 4/7

    AI that's already running

    "Who here already uses ChatGPT, Gemini, summarizers, browser extensions? With what data?" This is shadow AI: tools IT doesn't know about, often with customer data. It goes into the inventory.

  5. 5/7

    Data and access

    Where the data lives, who has the password, what can leave the company. Ask to see the screen, not to receive the database. Samples only without CPF (Brazilian tax ID) and without phone numbers.

  6. 6/7

    Next step

    Sum it up in three sentences and ask "is that right?" Agree on one task for the client (e.g., counting the week's orders) and the date for your feedback. No "I'll send you a proposal later."

  7. 7/7

    What comes out of the conversation

    The bottleneck with a number from today, the list of AI that's already running, and what can or can't leave the company. With that, lesson 3 can prioritize.

Talk 20% of the time. The other 80% belongs to the owner and the team.

02 · Four precautions that separate discovery from selling

Click on each one.

1. Case, not rule

"How do you handle service?" gets a textbook answer. "Tell me about the last order that went wrong" gets the real process, with the workarounds.

Gather three cases of the same kind: one case is an anecdote, three already show a pattern.

Good question"What was the last order that came back or was late? What happened, step by step?"

2. Pain with a number

The baseline is today's number, before any change. Without it, 90 days from now nobody can say whether AI helped.

If nobody knows the number, the client's first task is to count for one week. "I don't know, let's count" is a valid answer.

At Horizonte"On average, how long does it take between the customer's message and the quote being sent?" Patrícia Lima guesses "about 4 hours." Agree to measure 50 orders.

3. Shadow AI

Employees use AI tools on their own, sometimes with a personal account and with customer data. The goal isn't to punish anyone: it's to know what exists.

For each tool: who uses it, for what, what data goes in, personal or company account, who pays.

Signal"Juliana summarizes the service calls on a site she found" = customer data leaving without a contract. It goes into the inventory and to the Auditor.

4. Data with minimization

The consultant doesn't need the customer database to make a diagnosis. Ask to see the screen, and ask for counts and samples without CPF (Brazilian tax ID), phone numbers, and addresses.

If you need personal data, agree in writing on the purpose and the deadline for deleting it. Legal opinion is for a lawyer.

Ready-made line"Don't send me the spreadsheet. Show me the screen and tell me how many rows there are per month."

03 · Template: 45-minute discovery script

Print it or keep it open during the conversation. Replace what's between < >.

Discovery script
DISCOVERY · <company> · <date> · with <name and title>

[0-3] OPENING
- "My goal today is to understand where the work gets stuck. Whatever I suggest later comes from here."
- "I looked at <website / reviews / social media>."  - "May I take notes?"

[3-13] THE PATH OF AN ORDER
1. From the order to the money coming in: what are the steps, and who does each one?
2. Tell me about the last case that went wrong, step by step.
3. When you (the owner) are not around, what stops?

[13-23] THE BOTTLENECK (always ask for the number)
4. Where does a queue form? How many per week?
5. What work gets redone every week? How long does it take?
6. Where do you lose customers: before, during, or after the sale?
7. Is that number written down anywhere? If not: who can count it for 1 week?

[23-30] AI ALREADY RUNNING (no judgment)
8. Who uses ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, a summarizer, an extension? For what?
9. With what data? A personal account or a company account? Who pays?

[30-38] DATA AND ACCESS
10. Where are orders, customers, and prices kept? Who has the admin password?
11. Can you show me the main system's screen for 2 minutes?
12. What data can NOT leave the company?

[38-45] WRAP-UP
- Summary in 3 sentences + "is that right?"
- Client's task: <count X for 1 week> by <date>
- Follow-up meeting scheduled: <date>

NOTES: "surprised me" column | owner's phrases, word for word

| Tool | Who uses it | For what | Data that goes in | Account (personal/company) | Who pays | Risk (low/medium/high) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <e.g.: summarizer site> | <Juliana> | <summarize customer service> | <conversation with name and phone number> | <personal> | <no one/free> | <high> |
Data request (minimum necessary)
For the diagnosis I need ONLY:
- counts: orders per week, messages per day, quotes sent
- times: 50 orders with the time received and the time answered (without the customer's name)
- screens: see the system in a meeting, without copying the database
I do NOT need: tax ID (CPF), phone number, address, the full customer database.
If a need for personal data comes up: purpose and disposal in writing.
