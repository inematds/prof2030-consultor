02 · Templates: one-page report and rubric

Write the report last, after the attachments. Score it with the rubric before you deliver it.

One-page report (board)
AI DIAGNOSIS · Horizonte Distribuidora · <date> · <your name>

1. SITUATION (Carlos's words, 3-5 sentences, with 1 literal quote)
2. THE BOTTLENECK: <step> costs <R$/year or hours/week> (baseline: <value>, measured on <date>)
3. WHAT ALREADY RUNS UNCONTROLLED: <n> AI tools in use; <n> with client data -> action: <...>
4. RECOMMENDATION (just one): <opportunity> at the step <fixed rule / AI in one step / agent>
   because: <value> and <feasibility>   estimated cost per task: R$ <...> (prices as of <date>)
5. WHAT NOT TO DO NOW: <opportunity> - <reason> | <opportunity> - <reason>
6. 90-DAY PLAN:
   days 1-30 pilot (stop criterion: <...>) | day 30 decide | 31-60 stabilize | 61-90 measure ROI
7. HOW WE'LL KNOW IT WORKED: <result>, <operation>, <protection> against the baseline
8. INVESTMENT: diagnosis R$ <...> (done) | pilot R$ <...> | follow-up R$ <...>/month
9. LIMITS: does not include legal opinion or security testing; data risks go to <Auditor/lawyer>
10. DECISION REQUESTED: approve the pilot by <date>
Rubric (100 points, pass at 70 with no zeros)
| Criterion | 0 | 5 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bottleneck baseline | missing | client's guess | measured, with date and who measured |
| Inventory of AI in use (F12) | missing | list without risk | tool + data + account + risk + action |
| Value x feasibility matrix | missing | no calculations | 6 items, redoable calculations, discards with reason |
| When NOT to use AI | nothing discarded | discard without reason | >= 1 item solved without AI, with reason |
| Cost per task | missing | only token price | 4 tiers + human review + date and link of the price |
| Vendor risk | missing | generic | 5 questions answered for the winner |
| Pilot plan | missing | no stop criterion | 1 page, stop with name and deadline, exit plan |
| 30/60/90 dashboard (fixes F11) | missing | no protection | result + operation + protection, with before |
| Proposal | missing | no exclusions | exclusions, obligations, accept yes/no, fixed price |
| Report for the owner | missing | jargon or 3 recommendations | 1 page, 1 recommendation, no jargon, limits stated |

03 · Final project

Pull together what you did in lessons 2 through 7. Where something is missing, do it now.

E1 · The six attachments

60 min

Gather and review the attachments: discovery + inventory (F12), matrix, cost per task, pilot plan, 30/60/90 dashboard (fixing F11), and proposal.

Done when: all 6 attachments exist and every number has a written source (measured, from the lab, or simulation).

E2 · The one-page report

40 min

Write the report for Carlos Mendes. Read it aloud and cut whatever doesn't change his decision.

Done when: the report fits on one page, has a single recommendation, what not to do now, and the decision requested with a date.

E3 · Self-assessment with the rubric

20 min

Score your work with the rubric. For each criterion below 10, fix it or write why you couldn't.

Done when: the sum is 70 or more and no criterion has a zero. Then check it against the lab answer key.

Lab and answer key: inematds.github.io/prof2030-lab (the answer key is on a separate page, with a spoiler warning).

06 · What changed (Oct/2026)

Dashboard updated every quarter. The facts change; the method from lessons 1 to 7 stays.

09/22/2026 · Opus 5.5 cheaperAnthropic launched Claude Opus 5.5 at US$ 4/20 per million tokens, about 40% cheaper than Opus 5 for a typical task. Redo the cost table from lesson 4. anthropic.com
09/28/2026 · Sonnet 5.5, same priceSonnet 5.5 kept US$ 2/10 and, according to Anthropic, uses fewer tokens per task: the cost per task drops even without a change to the price table. anthropic.com
09/21/2026 · Grok 4.7 in the mid tierxAI launched Grok 4.7 at US$ 2/6. One more vendor in the middle tier: compare by task and run it through the risk questions. docs.x.ai
08/2026 · Open model that runs locallyAlibaba's Qwen3.8-27B came out open (Apache 2.0 license) and runs on a powerful computer, at about 17 GB in the 4-bit version. The "open local" tier became viable for an SMB with sensitive data. thenextweb.com
11/30/2026 · Agent Builder is retiredOpenAI announced on 06/03/2026 that Agent Builder will be shut down on 11/30/2026, with migration to the Agents SDK or Workspace Agents. This is the case for the pilot's exit plan. platform.openai.com
09/25/2026 · Vendor in dispute with the governmentA US appeals court upheld the "supply chain risk" label the Pentagon gave Anthropic. Vendor stability goes into the risk questions. cnbc.com
2026 · Agents are still a minorityGartner research: only 17% of organizations have AI agents in use, and agentic AI is at the peak of the hype. Diagnosis and measurement are still what makes the difference. gartner.com (Internet Archive)
